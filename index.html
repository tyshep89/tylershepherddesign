How I think about design

1Ask why before what

Shared understanding comes before solutions. I hold off on answers until the real problem is clear, even when everyone in the room wants to jump ahead.

2Map the whole journey

A screen is only one moment in someone's day. I map the full process, the handoffs, and the feelings along the way to find where people actually struggle.

3Design for the person doing the work

People want to do their job, not manage a process. Good design removes repetition so they can spend their time on work that matters.

4Tie every choice to an outcome

A design earns its place when it moves a result the business cares about. I connect features to impact so priorities are easy to defend.
